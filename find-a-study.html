Questions

What people ask before joining a study

What is a clinical study?

A clinical study tests whether a new treatment is safe and works. Studies follow a plan approved by an ethics board, and a licensed physician oversees your care.

Does it cost anything?

No. Study visits, tests, and study medication come at no cost. Most studies also pay you for your time and travel.

Do I need insurance?

No. Insurance is not required for most studies.

Can I quit after I join?

Yes. You can leave a study at any time, for any reason.

Is my information private?

Yes. The study team uses your answers only to check eligibility and contact you about the study.

Will I get the real medication?

Many studies compare a new treatment to a placebo or a current treatment. The screener explains how your study works before you decide.

Joining a clinical study is voluntary. Completing the questionnaire or a screening call does not enroll you in a study or guarantee eligibility. The study doctor makes the final eligibility decision. If you are having a medical emergency, call 911. If you are in crisis, call or text 988.
